Featured Data Science Project · In Progress

Mapping Charlotte's Changing Neighborhoods

A data science study of displacement risk and gentrification across Charlotte neighborhoods.

Research question

What housing, demographic, and economic factors are most strongly associated with displacement risk in Charlotte neighborhoods, and to what extent can changes in affordable housing, income, and housing costs identify communities experiencing gentrification?

What I am studying

I am bringing together neighborhood-level housing, demographic, and economic data to examine how rent, income, property values, development, and affordable housing availability change over time.

Planned analysis

The project will compare trends across Charlotte neighborhoods, identify relationships between key variables, and explore whether those changes can help flag communities facing greater displacement pressure.

Data sources

Planned sources include the City of Charlotte Open Data Portal, the U.S. Census Bureau's American Community Survey, and other public housing and economic datasets.

Visualizations in progress

Planned visuals include an interactive neighborhood risk map and comparison charts showing how housing costs, income, affordable housing, and development patterns have changed.
