Under Pressure: Housing Affordability and Displacement Vulnerability

An exploratory study of housing affordability pressure and displacement vulnerability across Mecklenburg County census tracts and Charlotte neighborhoods.

Final Research Question

Which housing, demographic, and economic characteristics are associated with rent burden across Mecklenburg County census tracts, and how do separate city data describe neighborhood displacement vulnerability?

How the scope changed

The project started with a broader question about displacement and gentrification. I narrowed it to rent burden and the City's separate vulnerability classification because those are the outcomes the available data can support.

Completed analysis

I cleaned and merged public data, compared housing and demographic characteristics across census tracts, examined their associations with rent burden, and reviewed neighborhood-level City indicators separately.

Data sources

The study uses the 2024 American Community Survey five-year estimates and two City of Charlotte geographic data services.

What the results mean

The analysis highlights affordability patterns worth investigating. It does not prove displacement, estimate individual risk, or identify neighborhoods that have gentrified.
