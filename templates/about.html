About me

Curiosity, code, and work that matters.

I like the point where an unclear question becomes something people can actually use.

Hi, I’m Kyle.

I’m a data science student at UNC Charlotte and a technology professional with the City of Charlotte’s Innovation & Technology Department. I continue to support Charlotte Fire’s Research and Strategic Planning work, where data, maps, software, and public service come together.

My work has included analytical modeling, GIS workflows, application support, automation, data validation, and data movement between enterprise systems. I work with Python, SQL, Snowflake, ArcGIS, Flask, Excel, and related tools to make complicated information easier to understand and act on.

I’m careful about what I publish. Many projects involve internal operations, so this portfolio explains the type of problem I worked on and the skills involved without sharing protected data, system details, or sensitive results.

What drives me

I see technical work as a puzzle. The first question is rarely the whole question, so I like tracing the data, testing assumptions, and building a path from uncertainty to a useful answer. The best result isn’t the most complicated model. It’s the one people trust and can use.
